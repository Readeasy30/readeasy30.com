Main Idea Practice

Find what the passage is mostly about.

The main idea is the big thought the details support.

Open Lesson AppMathEasy30

Simple main idea steps

  1. Read the title.
  2. Read the passage slowly.
  3. Ask: who or what is this mostly about?
  4. Look at repeated details.
  5. Say the big idea in one sentence.

Helper tip

If the answer is too small, it may be a detail. The main idea should cover the whole passage.
