What You Will Practice

Level A – Number Sense

Counting, comparing numbers, adding and subtracting within 10.

Level B – Operations to 20

Addition, subtraction, missing numbers, and simple word problems.

Level C – Place Value + Multiplication Intro

Tens and ones, skip counting, and what multiplication means.

Level D – Multiplication, Division, Fractions

Times tables, equal sharing, and understanding fractions.

Level E – Decimals + Percentages

Tenths, hundredths, decimal operations, and simple percentages.

Level F – Money, Measurement, Time

Counting coins, making change, measuring length, and reading clocks.

Level G – Ratios + Simple Equations

Ratios, proportions, solving for x, and reading charts.

Level H – Pre-Algebra

Order of operations, negative numbers, equations with variables, and multi-step problems.
