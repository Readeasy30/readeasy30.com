How to Find Sequence

Sequence means the order of events. Look for words like first, next, then, after, before, finally, and last.

Passage 1 Daily routine

Getting Ready

First, Lena packed her backpack. Next, she put on her shoes. Last, she waited by the door for the bus.

Questions

  1. What did Lena do first?
  2. What did Lena do next?
  3. What did Lena do last?

Proof

Underline the words that show the order.

Passage 2 Practical reading

Making Soup

Tom washed the carrots before cutting them. Then he added the carrots to the pot. After the soup cooked for 20 minutes, Tom turned off the stove.

Questions

  1. What did Tom do before cutting the carrots?
  2. What did Tom do after cutting the carrots?
  3. What happened after the soup cooked for 20 minutes?

Proof

Write or underline the words that prove the order.
