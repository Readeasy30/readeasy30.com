# ReadEasy30 Marketing Automation System

## Mission
Grow organic users through automated content, SEO, email capture, and social distribution.

## Funnel
Visitor → SEO Page → Free Practice → Email Capture → 30-Day Challenge → Return User

## Automation Modules

### SEO Engine
- Reading practice pages
- Comprehension pages
- Worksheet landing pages
- Parent/tutor resources

### Content Engine
- Daily reading lessons
- Vocabulary practice
- Questions and answers
- Printable resources

### Retention Engine
- Welcome email
- Daily practice reminders
- Progress milestones
- Completion recognition

### Analytics Events
- page_view
- lesson_start
- lesson_complete
- signup
- challenge_complete

## Distribution
- Pinterest learning pins
- Facebook education posts
- Instagram learning cards
- YouTube Shorts concepts

## Status
Marketing automation foundation deployed.
