# SEO and AI Search Standard

Use this repo standard for ReadEasy30 pages.

Every important page should include:

- One clear reading topic.
- A direct answer near the top.
- Helpful headings.
- Simple parent-friendly language.
- Internal links to lessons, worksheets, or related pages.
- A sitemap entry.
- An llms.txt entry when the page is important.

Keep the site plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Keep the calm reading-practice design unless Gerry asks for a change.
