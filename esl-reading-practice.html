Why ESL Readers Need Calm Practice

Learning English can feel hard when passages are too long or vocabulary changes too quickly. ReadEasy30 keeps practice short, clear, and repeatable so learners can build confidence one day at a time.

The goal is not perfect speed. The goal is to understand the passage, learn useful words, and answer with proof from the text.

Useful Skills for English Learners

Everyday Words

Practice words used in notes, schedules, directions, forms, and short messages.

Listening Support

Use Read Aloud to hear the passage and connect printed words with spoken English.

Comprehension

Answer questions by finding proof in the story instead of guessing.

A Simple ESL Practice Routine

1. Read the passage slowly.

2. Listen with Read Aloud.

3. Say important words out loud.

4. Answer the questions from the text.

5. Reread the passage and try again when needed.
