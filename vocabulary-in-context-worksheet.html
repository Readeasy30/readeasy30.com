What Are Context Clues?

Context clues are nearby words that help explain a new or hard word.

Ask: What words around the hard word give me a hint?

Passage 1 Everyday reading

The Weather Notice

The soccer game was postponed because heavy rain made the field unsafe. The coach said the game would happen next Tuesday instead.

Word: postponed

Question: What does postponed mean in this passage?

Clue: Which words helped you know?

Passage 2 Functional literacy

The Medicine Label

The label says to take the medicine with food. It also says to avoid driving if the medicine makes you sleepy.

Word: avoid

Question: What does avoid mean in this passage?

Clue: Which words helped you know?

Passage 3 School or work message

The Team Message

Rita finished the first part of the project. Now she needs to review the directions again before sending it to her teacher.

Word: review

Question: What does review mean in this passage?

Clue: Which words helped you know?

Proof Check

Underline the clue words in each passage. Then say the word meaning in your own words.

Helper Answer Key

Postponed: moved to a later time. Clues: would happen next Tuesday instead.

Avoid: do not do something / stay away from it. Clues: driving if the medicine makes you sleepy.

Review: look at again. Clues: directions again before sending it.

Helpful praise: “You used the nearby words to figure out the meaning.”
