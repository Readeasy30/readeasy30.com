How the 30-Minute Routine Works

1. Read

Start with a short passage. Read slowly. Use read-aloud help when needed.

2. Think

Pause for vocabulary, main idea, details, and proof from the story.

3. Answer

Answer short questions and review mistakes without shame.

Parent and Tutor Help

Helpers do not need to be reading specialists. A calm voice, short practice, and steady encouragement can make daily reading feel possible.

Keep It Short

Use one lesson or worksheet at a time. Stop before practice turns stressful.

Read Together

Take turns reading. Let the learner hear the words, then try again.

Praise Effort

Say what worked first. Fix mistakes second.
