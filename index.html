How the 30-Minute Routine Works

1. Read

Start with a short passage. Read slowly. Use read-aloud help when needed.

2. Think

Pause for vocabulary, main idea, and simple details. Rereading is welcome.

3. Answer

Answer short questions and look back at the passage for proof.
