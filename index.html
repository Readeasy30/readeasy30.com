Reading Should Feel Possible

Many learners need slower practice, smaller lessons, and a safe place to try again. ReadEasy30 gives one clear reading step at a time.

How ReadEasy30 Works

1. Start with a quick placement check or choose a level.

2. Read one short daily story.

3. Answer three simple questions.

4. Use Read Aloud, vocabulary words, and Bubbles for support.

5. Come back and keep building confidence.

🫧

Meet Bubbles

Bubbles is the calm reading coach who reminds learners to slow down, reread, look for proof, and keep going without pressure.

Who It Helps

✅ Children who need extra reading practice

✅ Adults rebuilding reading confidence

✅ ESL learners practicing everyday English

✅ Parents or helpers working with a learner

✅ Anyone who wants calm, simple reading support

Built for Daily Practice

ReadEasy30 is not trying to do everything at once. It focuses on a small daily win: read, think, answer, and continue.
