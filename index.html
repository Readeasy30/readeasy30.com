Meet Bubbles

Bubbles is the calm reading coach built into the lessons. Bubbles encourages readers to slow down, reread carefully, and keep going without embarrassment or pressure.

How the 30-Minute Reading Path Works

1. Start with the placement check or choose a level manually.

2. Practice reading for about 30 minutes a day.

3. Use Read Aloud and vocabulary help when needed.

4. Answer simple comprehension questions.

5. Track progress and continue building confidence.
