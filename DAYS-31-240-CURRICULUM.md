# ReadEasy30 Days 31–240 Curriculum

Full continuation path after the first 30 days. Day 31 starts Level B. Goal: practical reading confidence and 8th-grade reading readiness.

## Rules

- Level A = Days 1–30 starter path.
- Levels B–H = Days 31–240.
- 30 days per level.
- Keep lessons calm, short, and no-shame.
- Promise progress toward readiness, not a guaranteed exact finish for every learner.
