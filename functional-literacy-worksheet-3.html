Functional literacy worksheet 3

Reading Forms and Labels

Print this worksheet to practice everyday reading skills. Learners read a simple form and a basic label, then answer questions using proof from the text.

Functional literacyFormsLabelsAdult-friendlyESL-friendlyNo subscription needed

No subscription is needed. No paid tool is required. This page prints from your browser.

How to Use This Page

  1. Print the worksheet.
  2. Read the form slowly.
  3. Circle names, dates, times, and important details.
  4. Read the label one line at a time.
  5. Look back at the words before writing each answer.

🫧 Bubbles Tip

Forms and labels are full of details. Look for labels like name, date, phone, time, amount, and instructions. These words tell you what information matters.

Part 1Reading a form

Form: Community Class Sign-Up

Name:Rosa Martinez
Class:Reading Practice
Day:Monday
Time:10:00 AM
Phone:555-0148
Bring:Photo ID and notebook

Questions

  1. What is the person's name?
  2. What class did Rosa sign up for?
  3. What day is the class?
  4. What time is the class?
  5. What should Rosa bring?
Part 2Reading a label

Label: Food Storage Instructions

Vegetable Soup

Keep refrigerated.

Use within 3 days after opening.

Heat for 2 minutes before serving.

Contains: carrots, celery, and tomatoes.

Questions

  1. What food is the label for?
  2. Where should the soup be kept?
  3. How many days can it be used after opening?
  4. How long should it be heated?
  5. Name one ingredient listed on the label.

Words to Know

form

A page that asks for information.

label

Words on a package that give information.

refrigerated

Kept cold in a refrigerator.

within

Before a certain amount of time is over.

contains

Has something inside it.

serving

Giving or eating food.

Helper Answer Key

Form: Rosa Martinez; Reading Practice; Monday; 10:00 AM; photo ID and notebook.

Label: Vegetable Soup; refrigerated; within 3 days; 2 minutes; carrots, celery, or tomatoes.
