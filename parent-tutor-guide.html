The Best Way to Use ReadEasy30

1. Keep the session calm

Start with one short lesson. Do not rush. The goal is steady practice, not speed.

2. Read first, answer second

Ask the reader to read the story slowly before answering. Rereading is part of learning.

3. Ask for proof

When a reader answers, ask: “Where did you see that in the story?” This builds real comprehension.

What to Say When a Reader Struggles

Try: “Let’s read that sentence again slowly.”

Try: “Point to the word that helped you.”

Try: “You do not have to guess. The story gives clues.”

Avoid: “You should know this already.”

Avoid: “Hurry up.”

Simple 30-Minute Practice Plan

  1. 2 minutes: choose reader type and lesson.
  2. 5 minutes: read the story slowly.
  3. 5 minutes: use Read Aloud or reread together.
  4. 8 minutes: answer questions and find proof.
  5. 5 minutes: review vocabulary words.
  6. 5 minutes: celebrate effort and stop calmly.
