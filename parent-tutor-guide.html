Parent & Tutor Guide 🤝

Simple tips for a calm, successful reading journey.

✨ Remember: Keep practice times short! 30 minutes a day is all it takes.

🌟 Creating a Happy Space

  • Stay calm: Learners thrive when they feel safe and relaxed.
  • Praise effort: Celebrate the hard work, not just the correct answers.
  • Take breaks: If frustration creeps in, pause and return later.

📚 How to Use the Lessons

Have your student click on words they find difficult. The active speech helper will speak the vocabulary choices out loud to guide them effortlessly.
