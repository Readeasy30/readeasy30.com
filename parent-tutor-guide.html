Quick Tutor Routine

Use this routine on a phone, tablet, or computer. Keep it calm and short.

1. Open one lesson

Start with the lesson app or placement check. One small lesson is enough.

2. Read slowly

Let the learner read, listen to read-aloud, or reread with you.

3. Answer together

Ask, “Where did you see that in the story?” The proof matters.

What to Say When Reading Gets Hard

✅ “Let’s read that sentence again slowly.”

✅ “Point to the word that helped you.”

✅ “You do not have to guess. The story gives clues.”

✅ “Good effort. Let’s try one more small step.”

A Simple 30-Minute Reading Session

5 minutes

Choose the lesson and preview the title or words to know.

10 minutes

Read slowly. Use the read-aloud button or reread together.

10 minutes

Answer questions. Find proof in the story.

5 minutes

Review one mistake, praise effort, and stop calmly.

Helper Rules

Do

Use a calm voice, short sessions, rereading, and praise for effort.

Do Not

Rush, shame, compare, or turn every mistake into a long lecture.

Repeat

It is fine to repeat a lesson. Confidence grows through safe practice.
