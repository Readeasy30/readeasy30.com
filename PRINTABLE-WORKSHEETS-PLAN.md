# ReadEasy30 Printable Worksheets Plan

Printable worksheets are a future monetization and trust-building path.

Do not add payment, checkout, or live affiliate links until the free core is stable and partner or product choices are reviewed.

## Free worksheet ideas

Start with simple printable resources:

- Day 1 sample worksheet
- reading log
- vocabulary practice sheet
- main idea practice sheet
- proof-finding practice sheet
- parent/tutor session checklist

## Premium worksheet ideas for later

Only after the free site works well:

- 30-day printable reading pack
- Level A-D worksheet bundle
- adult practical reading pack
- ESL daily reading pack
- tutor progress binder

## Worksheet format

Each worksheet should use:

- large readable text
- short instructions
- plenty of white space
- no shame language
- simple answer areas
- optional helper notes

## Safe next action

Build one free sample worksheet page first. Use it to test demand before building paid packs.
