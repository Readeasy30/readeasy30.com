The 8-Level Reading Path

Level A: Short sentences, familiar words, and early confidence.

Level B: Longer sentences, daily-life vocabulary, and simple details.

Level C: Short paragraphs, sequence, main idea, and proof.

Level D: Practical passages, forms, notes, labels, and directions.

Level E: Stronger vocabulary, longer paragraphs, and summaries.

Level F: Nonfiction, context clues, compare/contrast, and evidence.

Level G: Grades 6–7 style reading with deeper thinking and explanation.

Level H: 8th-grade readiness: main idea, inference, evidence, bias, and synthesis.

Honest Promise

ReadEasy30 should not promise that every learner will reach 8th-grade reading in exactly 240 days. Starting level, daily practice, memory, attention, vocabulary, and support all matter.

Better promise: Many learners can build stronger reading confidence and work toward 8th-grade readiness with 240 days of calm, steady practice.

What 8th-Grade Readiness Means

It does not mean talking fancy. It means the learner can read, understand, and explain stronger text.

✅ Read longer passages without panic

✅ Find the main idea

✅ Use context clues for new words

✅ Find proof in the text

✅ Explain answers clearly

✅ Read real-life text like forms, notices, articles, and instructions

Move Up or Repeat?

Repeating a level is not failure. It is practice. The path should let learners move up when ready and repeat when needed.
