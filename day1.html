Read With Bubbles

Story

Tom went to the park. He saw a dog. The dog was small and friendly. Tom gave the dog water. The dog was happy.

Questions

  1. Where did Tom go?
  2. What did Tom give the dog?
  3. Was the dog happy or sad?

Bubbles Coach

Good reading can be slow reading. Point to each word, say it out loud, and take another turn when you need one.
