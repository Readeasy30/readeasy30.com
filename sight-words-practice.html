Sight Words Practice

Practice common words without pressure.

Sight words become easier when readers see them often, say them slowly, and use them in simple sentences.

Open Lesson AppMathEasy30

Simple sight word steps

  1. Look at the word.
  2. Say the word slowly.
  3. Read it in a short sentence.
  4. Cover it and try again.
  5. Stop before practice feels tiring.

Helper tip

Use only a few words at a time. A small confident win is better than a long stressful list.
