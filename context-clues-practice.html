Simple context clue steps

  1. Read the whole sentence.
  2. Find the unknown word.
  3. Look for examples or opposite clues.
  4. Try a meaning.
  5. Reread to check.

Helper tip

Readers do not need to know every word right away. They can use clues and keep thinking.
