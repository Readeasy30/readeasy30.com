# ReadEasy30 Level E Roadmap

Level E is the next planned reading level after the current Level A-D path is stable.

Do not add Level E directly into `app.js` until Level A-D quality has been reviewed and the owner is ready for the next lesson expansion.

## Goal

Level E should move carefully toward stronger practical reading skills without overwhelming learners.

The target direction is early 5th-grade style reading practice, then later growth toward 8th-grade skills.

## Reader skills to build

Level E should practice:

- longer paragraphs
- cause and effect
- comparing two ideas
- sequence and order
- main idea and supporting details
- using context clues
- reading notices, letters, emails, and instructions
- explaining answers with proof from the text

## Tone rules

Keep the same ReadEasy30 voice:

- calm
- plain language
- no shame
- no fake promises
- useful for adults, ESL learners, children, and tutors

## Lesson format

Each Level E lesson should include:

1. A practical passage.
2. 4 comprehension questions.
3. 4 vocabulary words.
4. At least one question that asks for proof.
5. One helper tip for rereading or context clues.

## Suggested Level E lesson topics

1. Reading a school notice
2. Understanding a work email
3. Comparing two appointment reminders
4. Reading a simple news-style paragraph
5. Following multi-step directions
6. Understanding a safety warning
7. Reading a public library flyer
8. Finding the main idea in a longer passage
9. Using context clues for a new word
10. Explaining an answer with proof

## Stop rule

Do not rush Level E into production. Build it after the current 30-day path is stable, reviewed, and easy to use.
