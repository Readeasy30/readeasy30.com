Reading Comprehension

Understand what the passage says.

Comprehension grows when readers slow down, notice details, and find proof in the text.

Open Lesson AppMathEasy30

Simple comprehension steps

  1. Read the passage slowly.
  2. Ask: who or what is this about?
  3. Ask: what happened?
  4. Answer the question.
  5. Point to the words that prove it.

Helper tip

Do not rush to the answer. Finding proof teaches readers to understand instead of guess.
