# ReadEasy30 90-Day Growth Plan

## Mission
Build a daily learning habit: 30 minutes a day from early learners through 8th grade and beyond.

## Priority Audiences
1. Parents of struggling readers
2. Special education families
3. Homeschool families
4. Teachers and tutors
5. Adult learners

## Month 1: Foundation
- Create lead magnets
- Build email capture paths
- Create parent resources
- Publish SEO pages

## Month 2: Growth
- Daily social content
- Pinterest resources
- Teacher outreach
- Parent community outreach

## Month 3: Expansion
- Partnerships
- Referral program
- Additional grade-level resources
- Community education outreach

## Core Message
Small daily practice creates lifelong confidence.
