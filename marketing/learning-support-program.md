# Learning Support Marketing Program

## Audience
- Parents of struggling learners
- Special education families
- Homeschool families
- Teachers and tutors
- Adult learners

## Core Message
30 minutes a day. One skill at a time. Build confidence through calm, consistent practice.

## Campaigns
- Reading Confidence Challenge
- Math Confidence Challenge
- Parent learning support emails
- Teacher resource outreach

## Positioning
ReadEasy30 and MathEasy30 support learners alongside existing school services, tutoring, and individualized learning goals.
