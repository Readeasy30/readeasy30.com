What Is the Main Idea?

The main idea is what the reading is mostly about. Details are smaller facts that support the main idea.

Ask: What is the whole passage trying to tell me?

Passage 1 Daily life Easy

The Morning Walk

Sam walks his dog every morning before breakfast. He brings a small bag and a bottle of water. The walk helps the dog use energy and helps Sam feel ready for the day.

Questions

  1. What is this passage mostly about?
  2. Name one detail that supports the main idea.
  3. Why does Sam walk his dog?
Passage 2 Practical reading Medium

The Library Notice

The library will close early on Friday for repairs. Books can still be returned in the outside return box. The library will open again on Saturday at 10:00 AM.

Questions

  1. What is this notice mostly about?
  2. What can people still do on Friday?
  3. When will the library open again?
Proof step Slow reading

Proof Check

For each passage, underline the sentence or words that helped you answer. Good readers look back at the text.

Helper Answer Key

Passage 1: Sam walks his dog every morning; details may include before breakfast, bag, water, dog uses energy, Sam feels ready.

Passage 2: The library will close early Friday for repairs; people can return books outside; it opens Saturday at 10:00 AM.

Helpful praise: “You found the big idea and used details to prove it.”
