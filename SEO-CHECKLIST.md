# ReadEasy30 SEO Checklist

Date created: 2026-05-28

Use this before publishing any new public page.

## Required page basics

Each public page should have:

1. Unique `<title>` tag.
2. Plain-language meta description.
3. Canonical URL.
4. One clear H1.
5. Helpful opening paragraph.
6. Internal links to related ReadEasy30 pages.
7. Footer links to Home, Lesson App, Privacy, and Terms.
8. Mobile-friendly layout.
9. No fake claims or overpromises.
10. No copied content from other sites.

## Search intent rule

Every page should match one main search need.

Good examples:

- daily reading practice
- adult reading practice
- ESL reading practice
- reading comprehension practice
- beginner reading worksheets
- reading levels beginner to 8th grade

Avoid vague pages that do not answer a real question.

## Trust rule

ReadEasy30 should not promise to diagnose, treat, cure, or replace a teacher, tutor, reading specialist, school plan, doctor, or licensed professional.

Use safe wording:

- practice support
- confidence-building practice
- parent/helper guidance
- daily reading routine
- extra reading practice

Avoid unsafe wording:

- guaranteed results
- cure dyslexia
- diagnose reading problems
- replace school help

## Internal link checklist

New content pages should link to:

- `index.html`
- `app.html`
- `parent-tutor-guide.html`
- one related reading resource page
- `privacy.html`
- `terms.html`

## Sitemap checklist

After adding a new public page:

1. Add the page to `sitemap.xml`.
2. Confirm `robots.txt` still points to `https://readeasy30.com/sitemap.xml`.
3. Confirm the canonical URL matches the sitemap URL.

## Readability checklist

Keep public pages easy to scan:

- short paragraphs
- clear section headings
- simple words where possible
- bullets only when they help
- one clear next step

## Monetization safety

Before adding ads, affiliate links, email forms, accounts, payments, uploads, or tracking:

1. Review privacy impact.
2. Update privacy and terms if needed.
3. Confirm the tool/vendor is safe.
4. Get direct approval.
5. Test mobile layout after adding anything.

## Final page check

Before committing a new page, check:

- Does it help a real learner, parent, tutor, or adult reader?
- Does it fit the calm ReadEasy30 voice?
- Does it avoid shame language?
- Does it keep Bubbles controlled?
- Does it preserve the simple HTML/CSS/JS stack?
