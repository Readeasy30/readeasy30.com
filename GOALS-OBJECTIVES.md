# Goals and Objectives

Date: 2026-05-27

## Main goal

Build ReadEasy30 into a useful reading practice site that helps learners improve step by step.

## Product objectives

- Keep the free reading practice strong.
- Improve lesson quality before adding flashy features.
- Support children, adults, ESL learners, older learners, parents, and tutors.
- Grow toward stronger reading levels over time.
- Keep the site simple enough for one owner to manage.

## Business objectives

- Build search traffic with helpful pages.
- Add printable worksheets after the free core is solid.
- Prepare for ads later without hurting trust.
- Prepare future premium resources after the basic product works well.

## Build objectives

- Preserve the current HTML, CSS, and JavaScript structure.
- Do not rebuild working features.
- Make safe improvements in committed stages.
- Update status and checkpoint files after stable progress.
