# ReadEasy30 Goals and Objectives

Last updated: 2026-06-06

## Main goal

Build ReadEasy30 into a useful, trustworthy, mobile-first reading practice site that helps learners improve step by step.

The site should stay simple enough for one owner to manage.

## Current product message

```text
ReadEasy30 is 100% free calm reading practice.
```

Use simple, respectful language for children, adult learners, ESL learners, older learners, parents, tutors, homeschool helpers, and community programs.

## Current visitor funnel

The homepage should guide visitors in this order:

1. Start Reading Now
2. Find Starting Level
3. Print Worksheets
4. Parent / Tutor Help
5. Reading + Math Plan

## Product objectives

- Keep the free reading practice strong.
- Keep the site calm, clear, and phone-friendly.
- Preserve the clean 240-day A-H reading path.
- Improve lesson quality after live QA passes.
- Support parent/tutor use without making the site complicated.
- Keep Bubbles calm and controlled.
- Keep ReadEasy30 aligned with MathEasy30.

## Current app objective

The public app should stay simple:

```text
app.html
student-profiles.js
app.js
```

Do not re-add old app script dependencies unless live QA proves a real current need.

## Business objectives

Build trust first.

Then grow carefully through:

- helpful SEO pages
- free printable worksheets
- parent/tutor resources
- practical reading support
- future premium printables only after the free core works well
- future ads or sponsor placements only if they do not hurt trust

## Build objectives

- Keep plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- Do not convert to React, Vite, Next.js, TypeScript, or build tools.
- Replace stale public files cleanly.
- Do not stack tiny repairs on broken repairs.
- Keep navigation simple.
- Keep parent/tutor access visible.
- Update status and checkpoint files after stable progress.

## Current next objective

Run live phone and desktop QA.

Fix only verified live-page issues.

After QA passes, improve worksheet pages and lesson quality in useful batches.
