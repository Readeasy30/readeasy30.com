Terms of Use

Use ReadEasy30 as Calm Practice Support

ReadEasy30 is a free reading-practice website. It is meant to support practice, not replace professional instruction or evaluation.

Allowed Use

You may use ReadEasy30 for personal reading practice, parent-guided practice, tutoring support, classroom support, and calm daily reading routines.

Important Limits

ReadEasy30 does not diagnose reading disabilities.

ReadEasy30 does not replace a teacher, tutor, school plan, doctor, or reading specialist.

Placement suggestions are practice guidance only.

Adults should supervise children using online learning tools.

No Harmful Use

Do not use ReadEasy30 to shame, pressure, embarrass, or punish a reader. The goal is steady practice and confidence.

Changes to the Site

ReadEasy30 may change, improve, remove, or expand features over time. Future resources may include printable worksheets, parent resources, tutor resources, or premium learning tools.

Terms Status

This plain-language terms page is a starting point for a simple educational website. It should be reviewed before adding accounts, payments, advertising, email collection, or user-submitted content.
