The Calm Reading Method

1. Read slowly. Do not rush the passage.

2. Reread one hard sentence. Rereading is normal.

3. Answer one question at a time. Keep the task small.

4. Find proof in the story. Look back before guessing.

5. End with encouragement. Effort matters.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Do not rush

Fast reading is not the first goal. Understanding comes first.

Do not shame mistakes

Mistakes show what to practice next. They are part of learning.

Do not do too much

One calm finished lesson is better than a long stressful session.
