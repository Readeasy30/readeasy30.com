---

# 🧠 CORE PRINCIPLE

Every lesson must be:
- simple
- short
- readable on mobile
- consistent structure
- beginner friendly
- supported by Bubbles AI

---

# 📄 STANDARD LESSON STRUCTURE

Each lesson must follow this format:

---

## 1. Title
Example:
Day 1: A Simple Story

---

## 2. Reading Passage
- 3–8 short sentences
- grade 2–5 reading level
- simple vocabulary
- clear story or idea

---

## 3. Vocabulary Section
- 3–5 words max
- simple definitions
- short explanations

Example:
Word: Friendly  
Meaning: kind and nice

---

## 4. Comprehension Questions
- 2–5 questions
- simple direct answers
- based ONLY on passage

Example:
1. What happened in the story?
2. Who was in the story?

---

## 5. Bubbles AI Support Section

Include:

- “Ask Bubbles” button or prompt
- Encouragement message
- Help instructions

Example:
"You can ask Bubbles to help explain any part of this lesson."

---

## 6. Encouragement Message

Always end with positive reinforcement:

Examples:
- "Great job today!"
- "You are improving!"
- "Keep going!"

---

# 🎯 LESSON RULES

- Never exceed 8 sentences in reading passage (early lessons)
- Keep vocabulary simple
- Avoid complex grammar
- One idea per sentence
- No overwhelming content
- Keep formatting clean

---

# 📈 DIFFICULTY PROGRESSION

## Days 1–10:
- very simple sentences
- basic vocabulary
- short stories

## Days 11–20:
- slightly longer sentences
- new vocabulary introduced
- simple comprehension

## Days 21–30:
- longer passages
- more thinking required
- still beginner-friendly

---

# 🤖 BUBBLES INTEGRATION

Every lesson must include:

- encouragement
- simple explanations
- ability to break down questions
- supportive tone

Bubbles is NOT optional — it is part of every lesson.

---

# 🧩 FILE STRUCTURE FOR LESSONS

/lessons/day2.html
...
/lessons/day30.html

OR dynamic system later.

By Day 30, the student should:
- read more confidently
