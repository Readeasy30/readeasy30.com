# Character Brand Guide — ReadEasy30

Last updated: 2026-06-01

## Official Reading Mascot

**Red the Reader™** is the official reading mascot for ReadEasy30.

## Locked Character Direction

Red the Reader™ is:

- female
- friendly
- encouraging
- calm
- smart without feeling intimidating
- patient with struggling readers
- safe for children, adults, ESL learners, tutors, parents, libraries, and adult education groups

## Locked Visual Direction

Red the Reader™ should usually appear with:

- red hair
- glasses
- red dress
- reading-friendly school look
- warm smile
- book, pencil, reading card, or worksheet prop
- clear, family-friendly design

## Brand Role

Red the Reader™ is not an open-ended chatbot.

She is a calm reading guide who helps learners feel:

- safe
- unjudged
- patient
- ready to try one small step

## Approved Core Messages

Use these messages when appropriate:

- 100% free reading practice.
- 30 minutes a day.
- Read slowly. Think clearly. Build confidence.
- Reading practice without shame.
- One calm lesson at a time.

## Required Website Address

Always use the complete .com address in marketing materials:

- `https://readeasy30.com/`

When cross-promoting MathEasy30, also include:

- `https://matheasy30.com/`

## Where Red the Reader™ Should Appear

Use Red the Reader™ on:

- homepage hero or welcome section
- reading app welcome area
- printable reading worksheets
- parent/tutor guide
- adult reading practice page
- ESL reading practice page
- Pinterest pins
- YouTube thumbnails
- short video intros
- outreach handouts
- library/literacy flyers

## Where To Avoid Overusing Her

Do not place Red on every section if it makes the page feel cluttered.

Best rule:

- use one strong hero image
- use small expression icons for tips or encouragement
- use worksheet/footer images sparingly

## Trademark / IP Note

Use the mark as:

**Red the Reader™**

Do not use the registered trademark symbol unless registration is completed.
