Privacy Policy

Privacy Comes Before Growth

ReadEasy30 is built to be simple, careful, and respectful of learners and families.

Current Data Use

ReadEasy30 currently uses browser localStorage to remember simple reading progress on the same device.

This progress is stored in the learner’s browser. It is not a full account system and does not follow the learner across devices.

What ReadEasy30 Does Not Currently Collect

ReadEasy30 does not currently require account signup.

ReadEasy30 does not currently collect payment information.

ReadEasy30 does not currently run live advertising code.

ReadEasy30 does not currently ask for private school records.

Future Features

Future features may include contact forms, email signup, printable downloads, ads, or premium resources. Those features should only be added after privacy, consent, and safety details are reviewed.

If those features are added, this policy should be updated before launch.

Children and Sensitive Information

Do not send private student records, medical information, addresses, passwords, or sensitive personal information through ReadEasy30.

Adults should supervise children using online learning tools.

Policy Status

This plain-language privacy page is a starting policy for a simple educational website. It should be reviewed before adding forms, accounts, analytics, ads, payments, or email collection.
