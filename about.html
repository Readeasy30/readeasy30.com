About ReadEasy30

Reading Practice That Feels Calm, Clear, and Doable

ReadEasy30 gives learners short reading lessons, simple questions, vocabulary help, read-aloud support, and steady encouragement.

Open Lesson App Parent / Tutor Guide

Why This Site Exists

Many readers need practice without pressure. Some are children who need extra help. Some are adults rebuilding confidence. Some are ESL learners practicing everyday English.

ReadEasy30 is built to make reading practice feel safer, slower, and more useful.

Who It Helps

✅ Children who need extra reading practice

✅ Adults who want a calm place to rebuild reading confidence

✅ ESL learners practicing short English passages

✅ Parents, tutors, and helpers who want simple daily lessons

✅ Readers who feel overwhelmed by fast or complicated programs

What ReadEasy30 Does Not Promise

ReadEasy30 does not promise instant results or replace a qualified teacher, reading specialist, doctor, or school support plan.

It gives simple daily practice, calm coaching, and a steady path for building confidence.

Start Small

One short lesson a day is enough to begin. Read slowly. Reread. Look for proof in the story.
