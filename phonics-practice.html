Simple phonics steps

  1. Look at the first letter.
  2. Say the sound.
  3. Blend the next sound.
  4. Read the whole word slowly.
  5. Reread the sentence for meaning.

Example

cat: /c/ /a/ /t/. Blend the sounds: cat.
