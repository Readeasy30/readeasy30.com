Reading Fluency

Read smoothly without rushing.

Fluency grows when readers practice short passages more than once and keep meaning in mind.

Open Lesson AppMathEasy30

Simple fluency steps

  1. Read a short passage slowly.
  2. Listen for words that felt choppy.
  3. Reread one sentence.
  4. Read again with a steady voice.
  5. Talk about what the passage means.

Helper tip

Smooth reading is not speed reading. The reader should still understand the passage.
