Beginner Phonics Worksheet: Short Vowels and Simple Words

Print this page for calm beginner reading practice. The goal is to say the sound, blend the word, read a short sentence, and answer from the words on the page.

Beginner phonics Short vowels Adult-friendly ESL-friendly

No signup is needed. This page prints from your browser.

Step 1: Say the Sound

Say each sound slowly. Do not rush.

ă
as in cat
ĭ
as in sit
ŏ
as in dog

Step 2: Blend the Words

Say each sound. Then say the whole word.

c-a-t
cat
m-a-p
map
s-i-t
sit
p-i-n
pin
d-o-g
dog
h-o-p
hop

Step 3: Read the Sentences

  1. The cat can sit.
  2. The dog can hop.
  3. The map is in the bag.

Step 4: Answer From the Words

  1. What animal can sit?
  2. What animal can hop?
  3. Where is the map?

Step 5: Proof Check

Point to the sentence that proves each answer. Reread the sentence out loud.

Helper Answer Key

1. The cat. 2. The dog. 3. The map is in the bag.

Helpful praise: “You looked back at the words. That is strong reading.”
