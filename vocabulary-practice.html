Simple vocabulary steps

  1. Read the whole sentence.
  2. Notice the word you do not know.
  3. Look for clues before and after the word.
  4. Try a meaning.
  5. Reread to see if it makes sense.

Helper tip

Ask the reader to use the word in a new sentence. Speaking the word helps it stick.
